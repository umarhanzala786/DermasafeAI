THE WORKFLOW

How Dermasafe AI works

A dual-AI pipeline connecting patient intake, image classification, intelligent insights and reporting.

01

Account & workspace

Users sign in with bcrypt-secured credentials to a dedicated workspace where their screening records are separated by email account.

02

Patient intake

The scan form captures patient name, age, gender, symptoms and an image for analysis with client-side and server-side validation.

03

CNN inference

The Flask backend loads the trained TensorFlow/Keras model, preprocesses the image to 64×64 and selects the highest-probability class from 5 conditions.

04

Gemini AI insights

The predicted condition is sent to Google Gemini AI, which generates detailed insights including causes, treatments, precautions, and when to see a doctor.

05

Record & report

The prediction and AI insights are stored in MySQL. Users can view scan details, export their history as CSV, or download a professional PDF report.

Model scope: The current project supports five classes — Acne, Melanoma, Peeling skin, Ring worm and Vitiligo. Results should be treated as preliminary screening support, enhanced by Gemini AI-generated clinical insights.
