THE WORKFLOW

How Dermasafe AI works

A focused pipeline connecting patient intake, image classification and reporting.

01

Account & workspace

Users sign in to a dedicated workspace where their screening records are separated by email account.

02

Patient intake

The scan form captures patient name, age, gender, symptoms and an image for analysis.

03

CNN inference

The Flask backend loads the trained TensorFlow/Keras model, preprocesses the image to 64×64 and selects the highest-probability class.

04

Record & report

The prediction is stored in MySQL and can be exported as a PDF screening report.

Model scope: The current project supports five classes — Acne, Melanoma, Peeling skin, Ring worm and Vitiligo. The repository documentation reports approximately 70% model accuracy on its dataset, so results should be treated as preliminary screening support.
